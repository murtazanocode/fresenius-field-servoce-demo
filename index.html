How each conversation becomes memory

When the technician closes a case, the conversation is turned into a structured case record. The next technician who asks a similar question gets that record alongside the manuals and past cases.

1. ConversationQuestion, photo, suggested causes, the sources cited and each verified step.
2. Case recordTurned into structured fields: machine, symptom, what fixed it, documents and revisions used.
3. StoreIn this demo, your browser. In the PoC, the CMMS case and a searchable index, with an audit trail.
4. ReuseSearched on every new question and shown as "From team memory" when it matches.
Demo only. Source documents are public FMCNA publications, used here to illustrate the approach; they are not modified. Past cases are synthetic. Answers propose and cite; the technician decides.
